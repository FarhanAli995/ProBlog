Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 12, 2026

ProBlog respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information:

  • Name and username
  • Email address
  • Password (encrypted)
  • Profile information (bio, avatar, social links)

Activity Data:

  • Posts, comments, and interactions
  • Likes, bookmarks, and follows
  • IP addresses and device information
  • Cookies for authentication and preferences

Uploaded Media:

  • Avatars and profile images
  • Blog post images and videos

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Authentication: To manage your account and verify your identity
  • Communication: To send password resets, email verifications, and transactional emails
  • Content Delivery: To display your posts and interactions on the platform
  • Security: To detect and prevent abuse, spam, and unauthorized access
  • Analytics: To understand how users interact with the platform and improve services

3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data based on:

  • Consent: You consent to our processing when you create an account or subscribe to communications
  • Contract Necessity: Processing is necessary to provide the services you requested
  • Legitimate Interest: To maintain platform security and improve user experience

4. Data Sharing

ProBlog does not sell your personal data to third parties. We may share data only in these circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Email services (Gmail SMTP) for transactional emails
  • Legal Compliance: When required by law or to protect our rights
  • Public Content: Posts, comments, and public profile information are visible to other users

5. Data Retention

We keep your data as long as your account is active. You may request account deletion at any time. We retain backup data for up to 30 days after deletion for disaster recovery purposes.

6. Your Rights

Under GDPR and similar privacy laws, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correct: Update inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Delete: Request permanent deletion of your account and data
  • Export: Receive your data in a portable format
  • Object: Object to processing for marketing or profiling

To exercise these rights, contact us at alyyfarhan4@gmail.com.

7. Cookies

We use essential cookies for:

  • sessionid: To maintain your login session
  • csrftoken: To prevent cross-site request forgery
  • Theme preference: To remember your dark/light mode setting

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but this may affect platform functionality.

8. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encrypted password storage using Django's hashing
  • HTTPS for all connections
  • Regular security updates and patches
  • CSRF protection and input validation

9. Children's Privacy

ProBlog is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe we have collected data from a child, please contact us immediately.

10. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
